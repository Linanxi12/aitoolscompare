## 3 Key Tests

### Test 1: Completion Quality
**Codeium:** ~10% behind Copilot on complex tasks, 32K context, fast latency. Competent for everyday coding. **Tabnine:** Slightly lower quality, weaker cross-file context in on-premise mode. Codeium's free completion quality is better.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Codeium — better completion quality for everyday development.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: Enterprise Deployment
**Tabnine:** On-premise deployment — model runs inside your own infrastructure. Zero code leaves your environment. SOC 2 Type II certified. **Codeium:** Cloud-only — code sent to external servers. Not viable for regulated industries. This is Tabnine's reason for existing.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Tabnine — on-premise deployment is a binary advantage for regulated enterprises.</strong></p></div>

**Codeium:** Unlimited completions at $0, 15+ IDEs. **Tabnine:** Limited free tier, requires paid for full features. For budget-conscious individual developers: Codeium's free tier is dramatically more generous.
