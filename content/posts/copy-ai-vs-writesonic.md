## 3 Key Tests 🔬

### Test 1: Sales Copy

**Task:** Generate a complete outbound sequence — cold email, follow-up, LinkedIn message, and call script — for the same B2B product. **Copy.ai:** GTM workflow generated the full sequence from a product brief. Messaging was consistent across channels, tone was appropriately adjusted per channel. **Writesonic:** Generated good individual pieces but required manual consistency checks across channels.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Copy.ai — GTM workflows are its standout feature.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: SEO Content

**Task:** Write a 1,500-word SEO-optimized blog post with keyword analysis. **Writesonic:** Built-in SEO scoring, keyword suggestions, meta description auto-generation, 78/100 optimization score. **Copy.ai:** Generated good blog copy but no SEO tools — requires external SEO tools.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Writesonic — integrated SEO is its advantage.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: Platform Breadth

**Writesonic** includes Botsonic chatbot builder (train on your docs → deploy to website) — no Copy.ai equivalent. **Copy.ai** has deeper brand voice and sales workflow features. Writesonic wins on breadth; Copy.ai wins on depth for its specific use case.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Writesonic for breadth (SEO + chatbot); Copy.ai for GTM depth.</strong></p></div>
