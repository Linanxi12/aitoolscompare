## What Synthesia Does

Synthesia, a UK-founded company, invented the AI avatar video category in 2019 and remains its enterprise leader. It converts text into presenter-led video.

- **230+ AI avatars** — diverse stock presenters, or create your own avatar
- **140+ languages** — script-to-speech with natural voiceover in most world languages
- **Script-to-video** — paste text, get a finished video with slides, images, and screen recordings
- **Templates** — branded templates for training, onboarding, product explainers, and compliance
- **One-click translation** — duplicate any video into 140+ languages
- **Team workspaces** — shared assets, brand kits, review workflows
- **Enterprise controls** — SOC 2, SSO, content moderation

## 3 Key Tests

### Test 1: Training Video From Script
**Task:** Turn a 500-word compliance training script into a finished video. **Result:** 18 minutes from paste to publish: chose an avatar, applied the company template, auto-generated slide visuals, and exported 1080p. The avatar's lip-sync and gestures were professional — slightly stiff, but entirely acceptable for corporate training. Traditional cost comparison: an agency quote for the same video was $2,400.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Script-to-video replaces agency work for corporate content.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: One-Click Localization
**Task:** Translate the training video into German, Japanese, and Portuguese. **Result:** One click per language — voiceover, avatar lip-sync, and on-screen text all switched. Quality of translation was strong; a native German speaker found one awkward phrase in 3 minutes of content. This capability alone justifies Synthesia for any multinational company: 140 languages without re-shooting.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>One-click multilingual video is unmatched — the enterprise killer feature.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: Avatar Realism Limits
**Task:** Create marketing content where a human presenter's warmth and authenticity matter. **Result:** The limits show. Avatars smile on cue but lack micro-expressions; pauses and emphasis are formulaic. For training and internal comms, viewers accept this. For brand marketing or sales pitches: the uncanny valley is still real — hire a human or use HeyGen's more lifelike avatars.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Professional enough for corporate; not for brand storytelling.</strong></p></div>
