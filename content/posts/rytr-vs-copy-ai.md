**Copy.ai:** GTM workflows — product brief → complete campaign (email sequence, social posts, ad copy, landing page). **Rytr:** Individual pieces, no campaign orchestration. For solopreneurs writing one piece at a time: Rytr. For teams producing campaigns: Copy.ai.
