## What Adobe Podcast Does

Adobe Podcast (formerly Project Shasta) was launched in 2021 as an experiment in AI-powered audio tools. It has since evolved into a focused suite of AI audio enhancement features:

- **Enhance Speech** — the flagship feature. Upload audio → AI removes background noise, reduces echo/reverb, and enhances vocal clarity to approach studio quality. One click.
- **Mic Check** — analyzes your microphone setup and room acoustics. Tells you if your recording environment needs adjustment before you record.
- **Studio** — a web-based recording and editing interface with AI transcription, filler word removal, and multi-track editing.
- **AI-powered transcription** — 95%+ accuracy, with speaker labeling and searchable text.

The core magic: the Enhance Speech model was trained on thousands of hours of professional studio recordings paired with their "degraded" versions (noise added, reverb added, low-quality mics). It learned to reverse the degradation — taking bad audio and reconstructing what it would sound like in a studio.

## 3 Key Tests

### Test 1: Speech Enhancement — The "Coffee Shop" Test

**Test:** Record 30 seconds of speech on a MacBook internal microphone in a café with background chatter, espresso machine noise, and street traffic audible. Run through Enhance Speech.

**Result:** After enhancement — the background noise was reduced by ~90%. The voice sounded fuller and warmer. Reverb from the café's hard surfaces was eliminated. The result wasn't identical to a studio recording (high-frequency detail was slightly softened), but was completely usable for podcasting, YouTube, or professional presentations. The "can I hear you clearly?" problem was solved.

### Test 2: One-Click Simplicity

**Alternative tools (iZotope RX, Audition):** Powerful but require 30-60 minutes of learning — noise profiles, threshold settings, EQ adjustments. **Adobe Podcast:** Upload → click "Enhance" → download. No settings. No learning curve. The AI handles all decisions.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Best-in-class simplicity. Professional audio tools are more powerful; Adobe Podcast is 100× easier.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: Limitations

**Music:** Enhance Speech is speech-only — it degrades music by treating instruments as "noise." **Very noisy environments:** Construction site, concert — still challenging. **Customization:** No manual controls — if the AI gets it wrong, you can't adjust parameters. For speech in typical environments: excellent. For edge cases: limited.
