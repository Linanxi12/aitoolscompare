## 3 Key Tests

### Test 1: Logos and Icons
**Recraft:** Generates editable SVG — clean paths, proper layers, scalable to any size, import into Figma/Illustrator. Unique in AI design. **Firefly:** Generates raster images (PNG) — good quality, non-editable as vectors. For vector assets: Recraft.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Recraft — SVG generation is a unique, unmatched capability.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: Commercial Safety
**Firefly:** Trained on Adobe Stock + licensed content, enterprise indemnification. **Recraft:** Standard terms, no indemnification. For client work and commercial products: Firefly's legal safety is a binary advantage.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Firefly — commercial safety is its defining advantage.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: Generative Fill
**Firefly:** Select → prompt → replace — industry-leading in-image editing. **Recraft:** No equivalent. For image editing workflows: Firefly's Generative Fill is transformational.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Firefly — Generative Fill has no competitor, including Recraft.</strong></p></div>
