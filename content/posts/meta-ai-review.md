## What Meta AI Does

Meta AI launched in 2023 and now reaches the largest installed base of any AI assistant — it's built into the messaging apps billions already use daily.

- **Chat inside WhatsApp/Instagram/Facebook/Messenger** — @Meta AI or a dedicated button
- **Standalone app and website** (meta.ai) — full-featured assistant
- **Image generation** — "Imagine" creates images from text in chat
- **Voice mode** — talk to Meta AI conversationally
- **Real-time web answers** — news and current events via search integration
- **Ray-Ban Meta glasses integration** — hands-free AI on the go
- **Meta AI+** — optional $14.99/month premium tier

## 3 Key Tests

### Test 1: Everyday Questions in WhatsApp
**Task:** Ask day-to-day questions in WhatsApp chat — recipes, travel tips, quick facts, translations. **Result:** Fast, convenient, correct on simple queries. Answers were shorter than ChatGPT's but accurate ~90% of the time on everyday topics. Translation quality was solid. For "quick question while I'm chatting anyway": genuinely useful. The frictionless access is the product.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Convenience is the killer feature — zero-friction answers where you chat.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: Reasoning and Analysis
**Task:** A multi-step financial comparison with conflicting data. **Result:** Meta AI produced a plausible but shallow answer — it missed one trade-off and glossed over the conflicting numbers. ChatGPT and Claude both handled the same prompt with noticeably deeper analysis. The gap is consistent with Llama 4's position: competent for casual use, not a frontier reasoning tool.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Fine for casual questions; falls short on deep analysis.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: Image Generation
**Task:** "Imagine" a product photo of a perfume bottle on marble. **Result:** Decent output — usable for social posts, but below Midjourney/DALL-E quality, with occasional text artifacts and flat lighting. The big limitation: images are generated and edited only in chat, with no professional tooling. Fine for fun and quick visuals; not for commercial design work.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Casual-grade images; not a design tool.</strong></p></div>
