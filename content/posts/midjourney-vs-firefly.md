## 3 Key Tests 🔬

### Test 1: Aesthetic Quality

**Prompt:** "A lone tree on a hilltop at golden hour, dramatic clouds, birds circling, photorealistic but painterly composition." **Midjourney:** Stunning — cinematic lighting, perfect composition, the "wow" factor is immediate. V6.1's aesthetic refinement shows. **Firefly:** Photorealistic, accurate, clean — but flatter lighting, less dramatic composition, more "stock photo" feel.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Midjourney — the aesthetic gap is real and visible.</strong></p></div>

### Test 2: Commercial Safety

**Firefly** was trained on Adobe Stock + licensed content → enterprise indemnification → you can use images commercially without copyright fear. **Midjourney** was trained on internet-scale data (including copyrighted works) → legal status is uncertain → ongoing lawsuits. For agencies creating images for Nike: Firefly. For personal creative projects: Midjourney's legal risk is lower.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Firefly — commercial safety is a binary advantage for business use.</strong></p></div>

### Test 3: Generative Fill

**Task:** Replace the background of a product photo. **Firefly:** Select → prompt → 3 variations in 5 seconds → seamless lighting match, correct perspective, proper shadows. Industry-leading. **Midjourney:** No equivalent. Must generate a new background separately and composite manually in Photoshop — hours of extra work.

<div class="verdict-box"><div class="verdict-label">📝 Verdict</div><p class="verdict-text"><strong>Firefly — Generative Fill has no Midjourney equivalent.</strong></p></div>
